What to Watch For

These report details can affect both scoring and lender underwriting.

High-Impact Areas

  • Late Payments Review every account for incorrect 30-, 60-, or 90-day delinquencies.
    Protect payment history
  • Credit Utilization Review both individual-card and overall revolving utilization.
    Reduce balances
  • Collections Confirm ownership, balance, dates, and reporting accuracy.
    Verify before acting
  • Hard Inquiries Make sure each inquiry corresponds to an application you recognize.
    Limit unnecessary applications
  • Accurate Positive History Confirm that current accounts and on-time payments are being reported.
    Preserve strong history

MWU Loan Reminder

The MWU Loan has a stated minimum independent-borrower FICO score of 660. Reaching 660 does not guarantee approval. The complete review may also consider payment history, credit depth, utilization, collections, defaults, delinquencies, and other program requirements.

If You Find an Error

Gather supporting records and dispute the inaccurate item with every credit bureau reporting it. You may also contact the company that supplied the information.
