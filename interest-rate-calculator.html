Private Loan Offer Analyzer

Enter a real lender offer or a sample private-loan quote to estimate monthly payment and long-term repayment cost. Your credit tier provides general underwriting context, but it does not automatically determine your rate.

!

Private lenders do not use one universal score-to-rate table. Two applicants with the same credit score may receive different offers because lenders also evaluate payment history, utilization, income, existing debt, loan term, program information and co-signer qualifications.

Before Accepting the Loan

  • Compare the quoted APR with the MWU Loan.
  • Review fixed versus variable pricing.
  • Confirm all co-signer obligations.
  • Review hardship and residency-payment options.
  • Borrow only the amount you need.
This tool provides simplified educational estimates. It does not model in-school interest accrual, capitalization, payment deferral, changes in a variable rate, lender-specific discounts or loss of borrower benefits. Private-loan approval and final pricing depend on the lender's complete underwriting review.
