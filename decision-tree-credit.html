720+

Strong to Excellent Credit

You may have several competitive borrowing options. Your goal is to protect your score and compare real loan offers carefully.

View my strategy ⌄

What This May Mean

  • You are above the MWU Loan's stated 660 minimum score requirement.
  • MWU Loan approval still depends on the complete underwriting review, including credit history, utilization, delinquencies, collections and other eligibility requirements.
  • Private lenders may offer competitive fixed rates to borrowers with strong credit.
  • You may be able to qualify without a co-signer, depending on the lender and your complete financial profile.

Your Strategy

  • Keep overall and individual-card utilization low—below 30%, with under 10% generally preferable.
  • Continue making every payment on time.
  • Avoid unnecessary new credit accounts before applying for education loans.
  • Keep older accounts open when practical to preserve credit history.
  • Compare the MWU Loan with several private fixed-rate offers.
  • Evaluate total cost, repayment flexibility, hardship options and co-signer terms—not just the advertised interest rate.
Likely next step: Use federal loans first, then compare the MWU Loan with actual private-loan offers. Excellent credit gives you choices; use that leverage.
Protecting a strong score matters because a lower score may move you into a less favorable private-loan pricing tier.

660–719

Building to Strong Credit

You meet or approach the MWU Loan score threshold, but targeted improvement may expand your options and reduce borrowing costs.

View my strategy ⌄

What This May Mean

  • A score of 660 or higher meets the MWU Loan's stated minimum score for an independent application.
  • Meeting the score minimum does not guarantee approval; all other underwriting criteria still apply.
  • Private lenders may approve borrowers in this range, but rates may be higher than those offered to top-tier applicants.
  • A qualified co-signer may improve approval odds or pricing.

Your Strategy

  • Pay down revolving balances and keep utilization below 30%.
  • Aim for utilization below 10% when preparing for a loan application.
  • Set up automatic minimum payments to prevent accidental lateness.
  • Review all three credit reports for incorrect balances, delinquencies or collection accounts.
  • Limit unnecessary hard inquiries.
  • Consider authorized-user status only when the primary account has low utilization, strong payment history and a long credit history.
Likely next step: After federal loans, evaluate the MWU Loan first and compare it with private offers. The MWU Loan's fixed pricing may be more predictable than score-tiered private pricing.
Lower utilization may affect a score after the card issuer reports the new balance. Broader credit improvement usually requires several months of consistent positive activity.

Below 660

Building or Rebuilding Credit

Your focus should be strengthening your credit profile and reviewing co-signer or MWU ACCESS Loan pathways.

View my strategy ⌄

What This May Mean

  • You are below the stated independent-borrower score minimum for the MWU Loan.
  • You may still be able to apply for the MWU Loan with a qualified co-signer.
  • Private lenders may require a co-signer, offer higher rates or decline the application.
  • The MWU ACCESS Loan may provide another pathway for students who receive a qualifying MWU Loan denial and meet the separate ACCESS requirements.

Your Strategy

  • Review your credit reports and dispute information that is inaccurate.
  • Bring delinquent accounts current and prevent any new late payments.
  • Pay down revolving balances and keep utilization as low as possible.
  • Consider a secured credit card if you need to establish positive revolving-credit history.
  • Consider a legitimate credit-builder loan only if the payment fits comfortably within your budget.
  • Avoid applying for multiple new accounts while rebuilding.
  • Discuss qualified co-signer options before your financing deadline.
MWU ACCESS Loan: This is not automatic funding. It is a limited, case-by-case institutional pathway for eligible students who cannot qualify for the MWU Loan or other available financing.
Likely next step: Build your credit, review MWU Loan co-signer options, and contact Student Financial Services to understand whether the MWU ACCESS Loan process may apply.
Some changes may appear within a few reporting cycles, but rebuilding credit commonly requires six to twelve months or longer depending on the underlying issues.
