This calculator provides simplified educational estimates. It assumes one equal loan disbursement at the beginning of each academic year, simple interest during school and the grace period, capitalization immediately before repayment, and level monthly payments thereafter. Actual lender calculations, disbursement dates, daily interest, fees, discounts, payment options, capitalization events, and variable-rate changes may differ.
