This calculator provides simplified educational estimates. It assumes fixed rates, level monthly payments and repayment beginning immediately. It does not model in-school interest accrual, capitalization, deferment, graduated repayment, borrower benefits that may later be lost, or changes in variable rates. The lowest modeled repayment is not automatically the best loan because federal protections, hardship options, co-signer obligations and eligibility requirements also matter.
